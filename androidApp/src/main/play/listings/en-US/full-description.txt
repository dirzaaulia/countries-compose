Experience our home planet and the cosmos like never before with Terra—an interactive 3D planetary explorer, real-time satellite radar, and space simulation suite.

Powered by a dual-layer hardware-accelerated 3D rendering engine, Terra transforms your device into a celestial command center. Seamlessly zoom from planetary orbits down to sovereign borders, track the International Space Station live, inspect active tectonic fault lines, and journey across the Solar System.

=================================
KEY EXPLORATION FEATURES
=================================

[3D EARTH & REAL-TIME ATMOSPHERE]
• High-definition 3D globe with photorealistic day, night, and cloud layers
• Dynamic solar terminator showing live sunrise, sunset, and solar angles
• Real-time NOAA SWPC Space Weather Auroral Oval modeling auroral activity at Earth's poles
• Bidirectional Planetary Time Machine: scrub backwards and forwards to observe solstices, equinoxes, and day/night cycles across any date

[LIVE SATELLITE FLEET & ISS TRACKER]
• Live orbital radar tracking the International Space Station (ISS), Tiangong Space Station (CSS), and the Hubble Space Telescope
• 3D Lock-On camera follow: ride alongside orbital spacecraft in real time
• Projected ground tracks, orbital apogee/perigee telemetry, velocity, and altitude

[TECTONIC PLATES & EARTHQUAKE HAZARDS]
• Real-time USGS global seismic data streaming recent earthquakes with Richter magnitude scaling
• Interactive tectonic plate boundaries illustrating oceanic ridges, subduction zones, and the Pacific Ring of Fire
• Volcanic hotspot monitoring and seismic depth analysis

[GLOBAL AVIATION RADAR]
• Live transponder-based commercial aircraft flight tracking powered by OpenSky Network
• Detailed airline route paths, aircraft origin/destination, and cruise altitudes
• Supersonic Mach flight simulation mode with dynamic vector wake trails

[3D LUNAR EXPEDITION & APOLLO SITES]
• High-resolution 3D Moon with accurate tidal locking and libration
• Interactive landing sites: explore Apollo 11 through 17, Chang'e, Luna, and Artemis target zones
• Lunar calendar & phase scrubber: track waxing, waning, and lunar age across the entire year
• Comprehensive selenological dossiers detailing lunar maria and highland geology

[MARS TERRAIN & ROVER GALLERY]
• Interactive 3D Mars globe featuring Jezero Crater, Olympus Mons, and Valles Marineris
• Telemetry and official high-resolution photo archives from Curiosity and Perseverance rovers
• Martian atmospheric pressure, sol calendar, and surface condition readouts

[HELIOCENTRIC SOLAR SYSTEM SIMULATION]
• Full Keplerian orbital simulation: explore Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
• High-precision planetary tilt, orbital periods, and retrograde motion modeling
• 3D Saturn Cassini rings with realistic lighting and axial inclination
• Deep-dive celestial dossiers: planetary mass, gravity, composition, and moons

[GLOBAL GEOGRAPHY, FINANCE & DOSSIERS]
• 250+ country and territory intelligence dossiers powered by REST Countries v5
• Live market status beacons across 85 major international stock exchanges
• World Bank economic indicators: GDP, population growth, and trade metrics
• True Size comparative overlay: eliminate Mercator distortion by comparing country landmasses side-by-side
• Interactive geography quiz: test your knowledge on flags, capitals, and landmarks
